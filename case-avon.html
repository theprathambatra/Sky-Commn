THE STORY / NOT THE KPI THEATRE

A collaboration works when two brands make the idea stronger together than either could alone.

The public story was simple: a bouquet with a perfume hidden inside. That is exactly the kind of idea the new website should celebrate — easy to understand, easy to feel, easy to photograph and naturally built for influencer storytelling.

01

Start with the human occasion

Mother’s Day provides the emotional reason. The brands should support it rather than hijack it.

02

Make the reveal physical

A hidden product creates a small moment of surprise that works in person and on camera.

03

Let creators tell it their way

Give influencers a clear idea and enough freedom for the reaction to stay believable.
